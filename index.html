About

I am a cybersecurity engineer and developer, and the founder of ZeroHook. I break things first, then build the compliant control that stops the next person doing it. Most of my work sits where attacking, defending and governance hand over to each other.

At ZeroHook I am engineering a DNS security and compliance auditing platform from scratch. The infrastructure is architected against NIST guidance, NIST CSF and NIST SP 800-30, and the platform measures DNS and email integrity and audits how it drifts over time.

To bridge technical security engineering and enterprise governance, I am preparing for the ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor certification.

On the offense side I do web, network and Active Directory pentesting, bug bounty, CTF and reverse engineering. My specialties are web exploitation (XSS, SQLi, IDOR, CSRF and SSRF), privilege escalation, and DNS and email hardening with SPF, DKIM, DMARC and DNSSEC.

I also build the tools: security tooling in .NET and C#, REST APIs, full-stack web, and ML work in PyTorch and pandas.

I am a competitive CTF player. With my team I compete in national and international events, solving real-world challenges under pressure. I reached the top 8% on TryHackMe at 16.
